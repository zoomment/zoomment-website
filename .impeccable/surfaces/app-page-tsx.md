# Surface: Zoomment site (homepage first; whole-site redesign)

Scope: whole-site visual replacement. Homepage `/` is the first surface (Persuade). Benefits and Donate are Persuade; Blog is Read; Auth and Dashboard are Operate and take the world's materials calmly (paper, ink rules, lettering titles) without performing panels.

Audience and job: indie bloggers and devs on static sites. Success = copy the snippet and install, try the live widget, leave believing it's free and open source. Must keep: the speech-bubble logo. Must not feel corporate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a Sunday comic page and every comment is a speech balloon. It refuses the dev-tool split hero (headline left, code right, feature-card grid below).

OWN-WORLD: Newsprint paper (#f6f1e4) under a faint process-cyan halftone; key-black brush-ink panel borders (5px) and gutters; process cyan (#00a6e8, matching the logo), magenta (#e6007e) for the one primary action, yellow (#ffe600) for caption boxes. Display: a heavy poster grotesk in caps; balloons: hand-lettered comic caps; body: a sturdy grotesk. Shading is Ben-Day dots, never offset block shadows. Balloons are ovals with drawn tails.

STORY: Visitor reads the strip left to right: your post, paste this snippet, readers talk back. They copy the snippet in panel 2, then scroll into the next panel and post a real comment in the live widget. Further panels prove free, open source, self-hostable, and no GitHub login.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink-ruled nav (logo + wordmark left; Benefits, Blog, Donate, GitHub, Login right). Masthead headline in poster caps left with yellow boxed key word, one-line promise right. Below, a three-panel strip at full container width (~560px tall): 1 "Your post" (magenta dots, blog page), 2 "Paste this in" (widest; dark code caption + magenta pill Copy button + "KLAK!" lettering on copy), 3 "They talk back" (yellow dots, three balloons). A drawn balloon tail line leads down into the live widget panel.

FORM: Balloon Press (comic strip newsprint), candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key 54194646. Raises kept: blank-balloon empty states (step row); isolated destructive actions (console); one tail line threading the page (Googie); lettering as structure for section breaks and states (alphabet storm); strict reading order (Versailles).

Signature interaction: Copy snippet → the button presses into the panel and a "KLAK!" sound-effect letters out with a short pop; caption flips to "Copied!". Motion grammar: balloons pop in once on first view (scale from 0.92, exponential ease-out), content visible by default; reduced-motion disables.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
